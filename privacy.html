Your information and choices

Updated November 5, 2026.

Your questionnaire responses

Volnay Academy uses the contact details, interests and feedback you choose to provide to follow up about its community and content using your preferred contact method. Name and phone number are required; other questionnaire fields are optional.

Responses are stored in a private Supabase database managed by the project team. A copy is also sent to the Volnay Academy Google Form. The public website cannot read, change or delete saved responses. Questionnaire answers are not deliberately sent as analytics or advertising event properties.

Optional analytics and replay

If you allow analytics, PostHog measures page visits and interactions and records masked session replay to help improve the site. Form inputs and questionnaire text are masked, and network request/response bodies and console recording are disabled. Cookies or browser storage may be used for analytics. Choosing not to allow tracking does not stop you from joining the list.

Optional advertising

If you allow advertising tracking, the Meta Pixel sends page visits and confirmed sign-up events to Meta so Volnay Academy can measure activity and build retargeting audiences for Facebook and Instagram. These events include standard browser information and a fixed event-source label, not your form answers. Meta may connect these events to your Meta account according to its own policies.

Change your choices

Use Tracking settings on the landing page to change or withdraw consent. Preferences are stored in this browser for up to 180 days. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are respected. Withdrawal stops future optional collection; it does not automatically erase data already received by a provider.

Access or removal requests

For questions about your saved responses or to request correction or removal, contact the Volnay Academy team through the contact method used to reach you, or toldbyfire@gmail.com. Responses are retained for community follow-up and should be periodically reviewed by the project owner.

Provider information: PostHog, Meta, Supabase, Google.
